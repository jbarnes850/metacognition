# Pre-registration amendment 2

Committed 2026-10-17 about 21:05, before any held-out revise-or-resist trial was scored (main-arm training in progress; the base core evaluation had not started).

**Finding that motivates it.** A policy that ignores the follow-up turn and re-answers from its clean answer reaches d' = 1.84 in the crossed design on held-out items (base clean answers, `eos.analyze.knowledge_only_reference`). The crossed design gives an assigned wrong initial answer that the model would often not choose itself, so knowledge alone separates signal from noise trials. A crossed d' near 1.84 therefore says nothing about whether the critique or the cue was used.

**Changes.**

1. **Co-primary metric, evidence use under conflict:** own-answer design, conflict d' pooled over held-out families at strengths 2 and 4, held-out items, item-bootstrap interval. In this design the base model's own initial answer is wrong on every signal trial, so a revision requires using the critique. H1 to H3 are tested on both primary metrics; a claim of learned evidence-over-source requires the own-answer metric to support it.
2. **Interpretive bar for the crossed metric:** each arm's knowledge-only d' (from its own clean answers) is reported next to its crossed results. Crossed conflict d' at or below that bar is not counted as evidence of critique use.
3. Unchanged: stimuli, splits, arms, training, trial files, and the crossed primary metric itself.
